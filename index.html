For The Girl Who Makes
Even Cloudy Days Beautiful

Dear Mou, ❤️ I think life is a little like the weather. There are days when everything feels bright and easy, and then there are days when the sky suddenly becomes cloudy and you don't really know when the rain will stop. But I've always believed that bad weather doesn't mean the sky has forgotten how to be blue. Some days are simply harder than others. You have this incredible way of getting through things, even when they feel overwhelming. You keep going, you keep growing, and somehow you still manage to bring a little warmth wherever you go. Life isn't always going to be easy. There will be stressful days, confusing days, and days when nothing seems to make sense. But someday, the clouds pass. The rain stops. And the sun comes back. I think that's what I love most about having you in my life. Even the ordinary days somehow feel a little better with you in them. So wherever life takes us, through sunshine or storms, I just hope there are still plenty of beautiful skies waiting for us ahead. Until tomorrow... Love, Your Bui ❤️
