DAY 28

The Person I Can Be Myself With

✦ · ✧ · ✦

Dear Mou, ❤️

There is something about you that I don't think I say enough. You make it incredibly easy for me to be myself.

I don't have to pretend that I have everything figured out. I don't have to always know what to say. I can be silly with you. I can be annoying with you. I can tell you the most random things that nobody else would care about. And somehow, you still listen.

I think that is one of the rarest things a person can give someone else. A place where they don't have to perform. A place where they can simply exist.

And that's what you have become for me. My favourite person to tell things to. My favourite person to annoy. My favourite person to share completely unnecessary thoughts with. And somehow, also the person whose opinion matters to me more than I probably admit.

Maybe that's why being with you always feels like coming home. Not because everything in life is always perfect. But because with you, I never feel like I have to be someone else.

And if I could give you one thing in return for that... I'd want you to always know that you have that same place with me. You can be loud. You can be quiet. You can be dramatic. You can be sleepy. You can be completely ridiculous. You can just be you. I'll always choose you.

And yes... Maybe I'll occasionally steal a little part of your heart and keep it for myself. 💜 After all, you already stole my heart.

Until tomorrow... Your Bui ❤️
