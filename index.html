My Favourite Story

A story I never want to finish.

Dear Mou, ❤️

You love books. And I think that's one of the things I love about you. You can disappear into a story for hours. You can become attached to characters who don't even exist. You can get emotionally destroyed by a few sentences written by someone you've never met. And I find all of that incredibly amazing.

But sometimes I wonder... If your life were a book, what would you call the chapter that brought me into it?

I don't know what page we're currently on. I don't know how many chapters are waiting for us. But I know one thing. I really, really like this story.

Because along the way, you stopped being just another person in my life. You became the person I want to tell things to. The person I want beside me when something exciting happens. The person I want to annoy when I'm bored. The person I want to run to when the day has been terrible. And the person I want to celebrate with when something goes right.

If I could write one sentence at the beginning of every chapter from now on, I think it would simply say: "She was there and she is still here with me."

And honestly? That's enough for me. I don't need our story to be perfect. I just want it to keep going. One ordinary day at a time. One ridiculous conversation at a time. One memory at a time. Until one day we look back and realise... we somehow wrote an entire lifetime together.

So yes, you may have hundreds of favourite books. But selfishly... I hope I get to be your favourite story that never ends. ❤️

Until the next chapter... Your Bui ❤️
