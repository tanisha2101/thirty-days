For The Hard Days

Some days just need a little softness.

Dear Mou, ❤️

I know today wasn't an easy day. And I know that sometimes things between two people who care about each other can become difficult. There can be misunderstandings. There can be frustration. There can be moments when neither person really knows what to say.

So I don't want this letter to ask anything from you. You don't have to reply to it. You don't have to make anything better right now. You don't even have to talk to me if you don't feel ready. I just want you to know something.

I'm here.

Not only on the days when everything is perfect. Not only when we're laughing together. Not only when life feels easy. I'm here on the difficult days too.

Life is going to be stressful sometimes. There will be days when work feels exhausting. Days when everything feels overwhelming. Days when we misunderstand each other. Days when we need some space. And maybe there will even be days when we don't know how to find our way back to each other immediately. But I don't think one difficult day can erase all the beautiful ones that came before it.

Roses aren't beautiful because they never have thorns. They're beautiful because they bloom anyway.

So if today feels a little heavy, let it be heavy. You don't have to carry it perfectly. Take your time. Breathe. Rest. And whenever you're ready, I'll still be here.

Because I don't want us to only know how to love each other when life is easy. I want us to learn how to stand beside each other when life isn't. And whatever happens tomorrow, or the day after that... I hope we remember that we're on the same side. It's you and me against the world.

There are going to be so many good days ahead of us. So many mornings we haven't woken up to yet. So many places we haven't gone. So many meals we haven't cooked together. So many stupid jokes we haven't laughed at. So many memories we haven't made. Today's difficult moment is only one tiny page in a very long story.

And I hope the pages ahead are softer. Pink roses. Warm evenings. Long conversations. Quiet hugs. And a lot of reasons to smile.

Until then... Please take care of yourself. And remember that somewhere in this world, your Bui is quietly rooting for you. Always. ❤️

No pressure.
No expectations.
Just here. 🌹
