For The Girl Who Keeps Going

Just something I hope you remember.

Dear Mou, ❤️

I think one of the most beautiful things about you is how strong you are.

You handle so much, keep going through so many things, and somehow still manage to be kind, caring and completely yourself.

There will always be days when life feels heavier than usual. Days when things don't go according to plan. Days when everything seems a little too much.

But I hope you never forget just how competent you are. You have overcome difficult things before, and you will overcome the difficult things that are still waiting ahead.

You don't have to have everything figured out. You don't have to be strong every second. You just have to keep going at your own pace.

And on the days when you forget how wonderful you are... I hope this little letter reminds you.

You are stronger than you think,
brighter than you realise,
and so much more capable than you give yourself credit for. 🌙

Always believe in yourself.
Your Bui ❤️

🌙

JUST IN CASE YOU FORGOT

You can do hard things.

Whatever life puts in front of you, I hope you face it knowing that you are capable, resilient and incredibly strong.

Keep going, Mou. ✨
