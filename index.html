🦉 Owl Post

Twenty-Nine Sunsets
Until Your Birthday

Dear Mou, ❤️ This morning, I imagined an owl landing quietly outside my window. It looked at me for a second, as if it already knew exactly where it had to go. Around its tiny little leg was an envelope with your name. I opened it slowly. Inside was just one sentence. "Tell her that not all magic lives inside Hogwarts. Sometimes it looks like the way she smiles." I smiled. Because I knew exactly what they meant. If Hogwarts really existed, I think the Sorting Hat would smile the moment it met you. Not because of the house you'd belong to... But because you'd make every house feel like home. Thank you for making ordinary days feel extraordinary. Thank you for bringing your kindness into my life. Maybe I never needed a wand after all... Because somehow... I found my magic in you. Love, Your Bui ❤️

One Last Thing...

If an owl delivered us two tickets for the Hogwarts Express... Would you get on that train with me? 🚂✨
