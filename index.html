Twenty-Nine Little Hugs

Dear Mou, ❤️

Today I was thinking about soft gentle things in life. And I kept thinking about you.

Because there is something about you that makes life feel softer. Not only because you magically make every problem disappear. But because somehow, knowing that you're there makes difficult days feel a little easier to carry.

I'm grateful for your softness. For the little ways you care. For the way you can make me smile without even trying. For the comfort of knowing that somewhere in this huge world, there is a person who feels like home to me.

If I could give you one thing today, it wouldn't be something expensive. It would be a giant teddy bear. Big enough to hug on the days when I'm not there to do it myself. And maybe a little note attached to it saying: "Whenever you miss me, squeeze this. It's the closest thing I could find to a hug from your Bui." 🧸❤️

Until tomorrow... Your Bui ❤️
