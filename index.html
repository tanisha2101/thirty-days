A little note, written somewhere between yesterday and forever...

My Dearest Nushi,

Some stories are written in books. Some are written across cities, through ordinary afternoons, unexpected conversations and little moments that somehow become the ones we remember. And then there are stories that simply happen. They begin quietly. You meet someone, you spend a little time with them, and somewhere along the way, without even noticing, they become a part of the way you see the world. I think that's what makes time so beautiful. It doesn't just give us memories. It gives us chances to understand each other, to grow, to change, to laugh at the smallest things, and to discover new reasons to care. Maybe that's what forever really means. Not knowing exactly what every tomorrow will look like. Just knowing that some moments are worth holding onto, some people are worth choosing, and some stories are worth reading again and again. And if I could choose one story to keep turning the pages of... I think I'd choose ours. ❤️

Always,
Your Bui
