Day 22

Time Has A Funny Way Of Making Things Beautiful

🕰️
Dear Mou, ❤️ I think time is one of the strangest things in life. We spend so much of it wishing that certain moments would come faster, while wishing other moments would stay forever. But maybe the most beautiful thing about time is what it does quietly. With time, we grow. We learn each other's little habits, our strange moods, the things that make us laugh, the things that make us quiet. With time, two people slowly stop being strangers and start becoming a part of each other's everyday lives. And I think that is what makes a relationship special. Not one perfect moment. But hundreds of ordinary moments that slowly become something important. Time teaches us to understand each other a little better. To value the little things. To appreciate someone's presence. And sometimes, time gives us something even more precious... The chance to become better versions of ourselves. So today, I don't want to wish for anything to happen quickly. I think some things are worth letting time take care of. Because the best things in life aren't always the things we find immediately. Sometimes, they're the things we grow into. And maybe that's the most beautiful thing about us. There is still so much time ahead of us. So many ordinary mornings. So many silly conversations. So many memories we haven't made yet. And somehow, that thought makes me smile. Until tomorrow... Love, Your Bui ❤️
